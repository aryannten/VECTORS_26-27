# VECTORS 2026 — Art Direction Concepts

This document presents three substantially different visual directions for the VECTORS 2026 website, based on the *Avengers: Doomsday* theme and the cinematic, premium requirements of the master specification.

**Please review and select ONE direction to serve as the foundation for the final design system.**

---

## CONCEPT 01: THE LATVERIAN MONOLITH

### A. Concept Name
**The Latverian Monolith**

### B. Core Idea
This concept focuses on Doctor Doom's role as a sovereign ruler and absolute authority. The website feels like standing at the base of an impossibly massive, intimidating fortress. It emphasizes monumental brutalist architecture, extreme verticality, scale, and unshakeable stability. The user is a small entity entering a massive, controlled domain.

### C. Mood
Oppressive, majestic, authoritative, cinematic, and awe-inspiring.

### D. Color Palette
*   **Base:** Deep Charcoal (#121212) and Gunmetal Gray (#2A2E33).
*   **Accent (Primary):** Latverian Emerald (#00FF66) — used sparingly for architectural lighting and critical interactive elements.
*   **Text:** Bone White (#F4F4F5) and Muted Steel (#8F939C).

### E. Typography
*   **Display:** A tall, monumental serif (e.g., *Cinzel* or *Ogg*) that looks like it belongs on a castle engraving or a high-end luxury brand.
*   **Body:** A structured, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Manrope*) for absolute clarity in event details.
*   **Behavior:** Very slow, weighty reveals. Letters don't bounce; they fade and slide in with gravity.

### F. Logo / Wordmark Direction
The VECTORS wordmark should be stark, utilizing the monumental serif font, perhaps with a subtle metallic sheen. It should look heavy, as if forged from dark steel.

### G. Hero Concept
The user is placed at the bottom of a massive, dark, brutalist wall. A single, colossal metallic structure (the "Monolith") towers into the darkness above. As the user scrolls, the camera slowly tilts up, and the VECTORS wordmark is revealed carved into or resting upon the structure, illuminated by a single beam of emerald light.

### H. 3D Environment
*   **Location:** The exterior of a monolithic stronghold.
*   **Architecture:** Brutalist, angular, vertical concrete and dark metal.
*   **Objects:** Massive pillars, heavy gates, structural supports.
*   **Materials:** Worn steel, rough concrete, matte black metal.
*   **Lighting:** Strong directional key light casting deep, hard shadows. Practical green strip lights embedded in the architecture.
*   **Camera:** Slow, controlled, track-and-pan movements. Very little rotation.
*   **Atmosphere:** Heavy volumetric fog at the base, obscuring the ground and adding depth.

### I. Event Browsing Concept
Events are presented as "Chambers" or "Vaults" within the monolith. Instead of a card grid, the user scrolls vertically through a dark corridor. Each event is a massive, dimly lit alcove; hovering (or scrolling into view on mobile) turns on the emerald "power" to that alcove, illuminating a cinematic visual for the event.

### J. Mobile Experience
Translates perfectly to vertical scrolling. The extreme verticality of the brutalist architecture feels natural on a tall mobile screen. Swiping up feels like riding an elevator up the face of the monolith.

### K. Animation Language
Weighty, slow, and deliberate. Transitions use simple, elegant masks and slow crossfades. There is no bouncy easing—only smooth, cinematic sine curves.

### L. Sample Assets
*   A 3D model of a dark, brutalist architectural pillar.
*   A high-contrast cinematic render of a heavy steel door.

### M. Risks
If the lighting is too dark, the site becomes muddy and unreadable. The brutalist nature could feel *too* static or boring if the textures and lighting aren't exceptionally high-quality.

### N. Why it fits VECTORS
It projects the scale and seriousness of a major technical festival, aligning with the "Doomsday" theme by emphasizing Doom's authority rather than just comic-book magic.

## CONCEPT 02: MULTIVERSAL FRACTURE

### A. Concept Name
**Multiversal Fracture**

### B. Core Idea
This concept focuses on the "Doomsday" aspect—incursions, shattered realities, and multiversal collapse. It feels unstable, high-stakes, and deeply cinematic. The website is a space where reality is breaking apart and reassembling. Information is revealed through the cracks.

### C. Mood
Tense, chaotic (but controlled), dangerous, high-energy, and sharp.

### D. Color Palette
*   **Base:** Pitch Black (#000000) and Obsidian (#0A0A0A).
*   **Accent (Primary):** Radioactive Green (#39FF14) — used for glowing edges and fractures.
*   **Accent (Secondary):** Incursion Red (#FF0033) — used sparingly for warnings, errors, or high-stakes events (like the flagship hackathon).
*   **Text:** Pure White (#FFFFFF) and Glitch Gray (#A0A0A0).

### E. Typography
*   **Display:** An ultra-wide, sharp, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Monument Extended* or *Clash Display*).
*   **Body:** A clean, slightly technical sans-serif.
*   **Behavior:** Fast, snappy, "glitch" or "slice" reveals. Text might appear to be split in half and slide together to form words.

### F. Logo / Wordmark Direction
The VECTORS wordmark should be wide and aggressive, perhaps with a deliberate "slice" or displacement running through the middle of the letters, separating the top and bottom halves slightly.

### G. Hero Concept
The screen starts as a seemingly solid sheet of dark glass. As the site loads, a crack forms, emitting a blinding green light. On scroll, the glass shatters into floating 3D shards. The shards catch the light and slowly orbit, revealing the VECTORS wordmark behind them in the void.

### H. 3D Environment
*   **Location:** The space between realities (the void).
*   **Architecture:** None. Abstract floating geometry.
*   **Objects:** Shards of obsidian glass, floating geometric debris, portals.
*   **Materials:** Highly reflective dark glass, emissive (glowing) materials for the edges of the shards.
*   **Lighting:** Stark, contrasting rim lights in green and red. No ambient light—only the glow from the fractures.
*   **Camera:** Dynamic, drifting, occasionally snapping to new perspectives.
*   **Interaction:** The shards subtly track mouse movement (parallax) or gyroscope on mobile, reflecting different lights based on the angle.

### I. Event Browsing Concept
The "Fractured Lens." Events aren't in a list; they are viewed through floating shards of glass. As the user scrolls, a massive shard floats into the center of the screen, acting as a magnifying glass or portal that reveals the cinematic image and details for that specific event.

### J. Mobile Experience
The floating shards work well in a vertical stack. Swiping snaps the next shard into focus. The use of true black will look stunning on mobile OLED screens, saving battery and making the glowing accents pop.

### K. Animation Language
Snappy, elastic, and sometimes jarring (in a controlled way). Uses custom easing that starts fast and snaps into place, mimicking the sudden breaking of glass or a glitch in reality.

### L. Sample Assets
*   A 3D render of shattered, reflective obsidian glass.
*   A particle system of glowing green embers.

### M. Risks
Can easily become visually overwhelming or induce motion sickness if the shards move too much. The "glitch" aesthetic can look cheap or dated if not executed with extreme polish.

### N. Why it fits VECTORS
It perfectly captures the disruptive, high-energy nature of a tech festival and hackathons, directly tying into the multiverse-collapse aspect of Doomsday.

## CONCEPT 03: THE ARCANE MACHINE

### A. Concept Name
**The Arcane Machine**

### B. Core Idea
This concept focuses on Doom's unique blend of advanced technology and dark magic. The website feels like the inside of a massive, ancient, but highly advanced machine—an astrolabe, a reactor, or a mystical engine. It is intricate, complex, and deeply layered.

### C. Mood
Mysterious, intellectual, complex, historic-yet-futuristic, and precise.

### D. Color Palette
*   **Base:** Rusted Iron (#1C1A17) and Dark Bronze (#2D241E).
*   **Accent (Primary):** Toxic/Magic Green (#10B981) — for glowing runes and energy cores.
*   **Accent (Secondary):** Tarnished Gold/Brass (#B89C49) — for UI elements and borders.
*   **Text:** Pale Parchment (#F9F6EE) and Dim Brass (#A39A82).

### E. Typography
*   **Display:** A high-contrast, sharp serif with almost gothic or arcane undertones (e.g., *Playfair Display* or a modern blackletter variant).
*   **Body:** A monospaced, technical font (e.g., *JetBrains Mono* or *Space Mono*) representing the "machine" output.
*   **Behavior:** Text decodes or scrambles into place, like an ancient cipher being solved by a computer.

### F. Logo / Wordmark Direction
An intricate emblem. The VECTORS wordmark could be encased in a circular, astrolabe-like seal, combining sharp geometric lines with arcane-looking circuitry patterns.

### G. Hero Concept
The user is looking down the barrel of a massive, dormant mechanical core. Concentric rings of dark metal cover the screen. As the user scrolls, the rings unlock, rotate in opposite directions, and recess backward, revealing the glowing green VECTORS core in the center.

### H. 3D Environment
*   **Location:** The interior of a world-engine.
*   **Architecture:** Cylindrical, layered, mechanical.
*   **Objects:** Gears, concentric rings, interlocking panels, glowing circuitry that looks like magical runes.
*   **Materials:** Tarnished brass, dark copper, rusted iron, and glowing neon tubes.
*   **Lighting:** Centralized glow from the "core" (green), with soft amber rim lights catching the metallic edges of the gears.
*   **Camera:** Mostly Z-axis movement (pushing inward/forward) through layers of machinery.

### I. Event Browsing Concept
The "Cipher Lock." Events are presented as interlocking dials on a massive safe. Scrolling vertically rotates the dials. When the dials align in the center of the screen, the safe "unlocks" to display the event details and cinematic imagery.

### J. Mobile Experience
The concentric circle/dial motif translates perfectly to a mobile thumb-wheel interaction. Scrolling feels like turning a combination lock to reveal the next event.

### K. Animation Language
Mechanical, precise, and interlocking. Easing curves should mimic the physical weight of heavy gears turning and snapping into locked positions.

### L. Sample Assets
*   A 3D model of an intricate, brass-and-iron astrolabe ring.
*   A seamless texture of circuitry that resembles arcane runes.

### M. Risks
The intricate details might get lost or look muddy on smaller mobile screens. The steampunk/brass aesthetic can sometimes look dated if the green "magic" energy isn't modern and vibrant enough to contrast it.

### N. Why it fits VECTORS
It beautifully marries the two sides of a tech festival: the hard, physical engineering (the machine) and the cutting-edge, almost magical software/AI side (the glowing runes). It honors the Doomsday character perfectly.
